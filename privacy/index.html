Busted

Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Busted is an iPhone app that locks apps once you go over a daily limit you set. To unlock one, you take a selfie, which your friends see. This policy explains what Busted collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Who runs Busted

Busted is run by Jodh Rai, an individual based in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). We are the controller of your personal data under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Contact: busted.support.help@gmail.com

Screen Time stays on your phone

Busted uses Apple's Screen Time features to watch the apps you choose. Apple doesn't let Busted see which apps you use, what you do in them or how long you spend in them. The app only learns that an app you picked has reached one of your limits. Your app choices and limits are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us.

Busted has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and doesn't sell or share your data for advertising. We don't track you across other apps or websites.

When you take a selfie, the app finds your face on your phone to frame the photo. This happens only on the device: no face data is stored or sent to us.

Who can see it

  • Your friends (people whose request you accepted, or who accepted yours) can see your username and your posts. They see each post for about two days, and can react to it.
  • Anyone using Busted can find you by searching your exact username.
  • You can see your own posts for up to a year in the app.
  • Us: we look at posts and accounts only when they've been reported, to keep the app safe, or when the law requires it.
  • Our service providers: Supabase stores the app's data and photos for us, and Apple provides sign-in and the App Store. They process data only on our instructions or under their own privacy policies.

Posts that 3 people report are hidden from everyone. We may also remove content or accounts that break the Terms of Use.

Legal bases

We use your data to provide the service you signed up for (contract), and to keep Busted safe and secure, for example handling reports and blocks and preventing abuse (legitimate interests).

Where it's stored

Our data is hosted by Supabase, whose servers may be outside the UK. Where data is transferred outside the UK, it's protected by the safeguards in Supabase's data processing agreement, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and posts until you delete your account. When you do, we delete your profile, posts, photos, reactions, friends, blocks and the reports you made, straight away. Server logs are kept for a short period and then deleted automatically. Removing the app from your phone doesn't delete your account, so delete your account in the app first.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Friends, scroll down to Account and tap Delete account. Apple asks you to confirm, and we then tell Apple to stop Busted using Sign in with Apple for your Apple ID, so Busted no longer appears in your Apple ID's Sign in with Apple settings. If you can't use the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Under UK law you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to move it to another service. Email us and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Age

Busted is only for people aged 18 or over. If we learn that someone under 18 is using it, we'll delete their account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For big changes, we'll tell you in the app.
